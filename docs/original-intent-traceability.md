# Original-Intent Traceability

**Status:** Governing scope trace derived from founding sources and accepted
decisions. It is not a delivery roadmap and does not claim implementation.

**Purpose:** Keep noetic-dev's complete end visible while work is sequenced, and
make false completion legible before a bounded integration is mistaken for the
whole framework.

## 1. Prime directive

Build and progressively harden **one portable development-and-cognitive
framework that operationalizes authentic inquiry and responsible action**. Its
recurrent machine-facing v1 constitution is P1-P4; its end remains humanly
authorized; Telos durably mediates that purpose; a runtime-neutral deterministic
controller executes typed work through versioned cognitive programs; every
implementation generation receives exactly one independent adversarial QA pass;
attributed causal events and provenance-preserving semantic session memory make
the work replayable and inspectable; read-only-first observability and swappable
attach surfaces expose it; explicit runtime, client, workspace, effect, and model
adapters keep it portable; genus-router selection and LiteLLM access govern local
generative, vision, embedding, and ASR capability; SAE/GEH research remains an
active, empirically qualified feedback track; verified donor invariants are
selectively re-instantiated rather than copied wholesale; and this thin
composition root proves clean composition, recovery, rollback, and real
end-to-end operation. ContextForge and every other infrastructure choice remain
replaceable means, never the end or the backbone.

This directive restates `initial-user-msg.md`, the invariant founding charge in
`AGENTS.md`, the adjudicated architecture in `SYNTHESIS.md`, and accepted
decisions `dec-20260801-0001` through `dec-20260801-0005`. Sequencing,
non-gating status, or a blocked dependency does not make any original-intent
dimension abeyant.

### 3.2 Architectural distinctions

- **Telos/end:** the worthwhile end judged and authorized by a human. It is not a
  repository, server, queue, metric, or passing test.
- **P1-P4 cognitive constitution:** the recurrent form `P1 attend -> P2
  understand -> P3 judge -> P4 decide`, recursively governed by responsible
  deliberation and decision. It is a cognitional and evaluative grammar, not a
  claim that every state transition is cognition.
- **Human and agent operations:** humans remain the responsible subjects who
  authorize the end and finally adjudicate value. Agents may perform and report
  operations attributed to them under the P1-P4 vocabulary; those reports are
  evidence, not self-authenticating truth or proof of consciousness. This
  preserves the qualification in
  `/home/dgk/workspace/saeproj/docs/foundational/self_appropriation.md`.
- **Telos teleological mediation:** the Telos component persists authorized
  goals, principles, reproductive clauses, continuation, delegation, and
  completion evidence. It mediates and steers purpose; it is not the responsible
  subject and does not turn stored intent into authentic responsibility.
- **Deterministic executive controller:** enforces typed dependency legality,
  waves, ordinals, lifecycle, retries, cancellation, QA accounting, effect
  boundaries, and recovery. It controls how and when; it never asserts semantic
  truth or substitutes deterministic mechanics for P3 judgment.
- **Cognitive programs:** versioned disciplines such as phronesis,
  differentiated cognition, and emergent-probability inquiry that direct agents
  through semantic operations on the controller. They are programs, not the
  controller itself.
- **Development pipeline:** `development_pipeline.v1` is the particular cognitive
  program with bounded QA/remediation cycles and exactly one independent
  adversarial QA pass per frozen implementation generation. It is not the
  totality of cognitive programs.
- **Attributed events:** append-only causal evidence naming actor, principal,
  operation, input/output references, component/version, parentage, time,
  authority, and outcome. An event that says "judged" remains an attributed
  report until separately judged.
- **Semantic session memory:** provenance-preserving embedding, summarization,
  retention, and search over sessions and events. It is a derived retrieval
  layer, not the causal event authority, and must retain links back to evidence.
- **Observability/control inquiry:** replay-derived projections expose state,
  causal history, readiness, blocks, census, memory, and QA lineage. The first
  surface is read-only; whether a later surface may command effects remains an
  authority question, not a UI convenience.
- **Attach:** a swappable way to watch or enter a live agent, with tmux-native
  attach the default direction and browser PTY optional. Attach is neither the
  event model nor the control plane.
- **Runtime/client adapters:** OpenCode, Pi, Codex, Claude, Goose, VS Code, and
  future clients are replaceable surfaces behind contracts. Workspace, effect,
  persistence, and model adapters likewise isolate environmental mechanics from
  the cognitive and teleological core.
- **Inference substrate:** genus-router honestly selects by task genus; LiteLLM
  is the universal model-access boundary; local servers provide generative,
  coding/reasoning, vision, embedding, and ASR capabilities. This substrate
  supplies instruments, not purpose or cognition.
- **SAE/GEH research:** saeproj investigates whether P1-P4 operation patterns
  have content-invariant representation geometry and whether training can make
  it explicit. The research has a feedback seam into programs, evaluation, and
  future models, but its empirical hypotheses carry lower and separate
  confidence from the cognitional constitution.
- **Donor harvesting:** noetic-pi, pi2, Telos, cognitive-disciplines, and other
  experiments provide contracts, invariants, fixtures, and lessons. Harvest
  means characterize, pin, test, and re-instantiate a superior portable form;
  donor maturity never makes a donor the framework.
- **Thin composition and recovery:** this repository owns canonical contracts,
  pins, configuration, composition, integration proof, replay, migration,
  rollback, and recovery. Independently testable components stay in their own
  homes; a local happy path is not recoverable composition.
- **Governance/publication:** issues, branches, review, exact implementation-to-QA
  pairing, protected release, licensing, and publication evidence govern
  delivery. They condition responsible release but are not the cognitive
  constitution or proof that the runtime exists.
- **ContextForge optional transport:** compatibility and multi-host distribution
  transport only. Plain MCP stdio/HTTP is the default; ContextForge is not the
  composition root, event authority, controller, teleological governor, or a
  complete-system success criterion.

## 4. Load-bearing vertical slice

The first authentic slice must exercise this entire path with real persisted
evidence:

```text
human authorization
  -> Telos goalchain
  -> typed dispatch
  -> deterministic runtime-neutral controller
  -> minimal versioned cognitive program
  -> bounded effect + frozen implementation generation
  -> exactly one independent adversarial QA
  -> attributed event + provenance-preserving semantic memory
  -> separate critical judgment + responsible adjudication
  -> replay + recovery + rollback
```

The minimal program in this first slice is not represented as a partial
`development_pipeline.v1`; that identifier is reserved for an execution that
contains all four canonical phases in section 3.1.

The slice must include a bounded real effect, explicit component versions,
idempotent admission, retry/duplicate handling, cancellation, crash/restart,
separate implementer and QA identities, and a result that Telos can reconcile
without claiming impossible cross-system exactly-once execution. A fixture,
synthetic trace, prompt transcript, or controller-only schedule cannot substitute
for this path.

## 8. Complete-system success gates

Noetic-dev is complete only when all of these gates hold together against exact,
versioned component identities:

1. **End and constitution:** a humanly authorized end governs a real execution;
   P1-P4 operations remain differentiated and attributed; Telos is demonstrably a
   mediator rather than a claimed responsible subject.
2. **Executable spine:** the full load-bearing vertical slice in section 4 runs
   with typed dispatch, one minimal cognitive program, one real bounded effect,
   exactly one independent QA, separate judgment/adjudication, and persisted
   evidence.
3. **Deterministic control without semantic capture:** legality, lifecycle,
   retries, duplicates, cancellation, exhaustion, and recovery are deterministic;
   semantic conclusions remain separately attributable and judged.
4. **Programs and pipeline:** at least one discipline and the complete
   `development_pipeline.v1`, with all four canonical phases from section 3.1,
   run through common contracts over fake and reference runtimes without donor
   coupling.
5. **Events and memory:** real append-only causal events replay equivalently;
   semantic session embedding, summarization, retention, and search preserve
   provenance, temporal scope, privacy, and uncertainty across restart.
6. **Observation and attach:** read-only web or TUI projections explain live and
   replayed causal state from real events; attach remains replaceable and passes
   identity, reconnect, isolation, and teardown drills.
7. **Portability:** the authentic slice conforms on at least two independent
   runtime/client adapters; one client is insufficient regardless of quality.
8. **Multimodal substrate:** governed real generative/reasoning, vision,
   embedding, and ASR tasks pass through honest genus-router selection and the
   LiteLLM boundary with outcome feedback and bounded failure behavior.
9. **Composition and survival:** a clean environment resolves exact pins and
   passes compatibility, restart, forced retry/duplicate, crash recovery, replay,
   upgrade, rollback, and known-good restoration. `noetic-pi-docker` remains
   traced as the prior developed product instance, not the composition basis or
   proof that noetic-dev is complete.
10. **Research honesty:** philosophical/cognitional conformance and SAE/GEH
    empirical support are reported as different claims with different confidence;
    unproven representation or training hypotheses are not smuggled into the
    constitution or production claims.
11. **Responsible release:** accepted scope, exact implementation-to-QA evidence,
    current security and authority adjudication, licensing, protected release,
    immutable version identity, and rollback evidence bind the released whole.

## 9. Anti-drift checks

- ContextForge or a Pi/OpenCode harness being green is insufficient: it proves a
  bounded adapter path, not the end, cognitive constitution, controller, memory,
  portability, or recovery.
- Governance being green is insufficient: it proves delivery controls, not an
  authentic runtime or complete capability portfolio.
- Synthetic traces being green are insufficient: they prove contract fixtures,
  not real authority, effects, event causality, semantic memory, or recovery.
- One client being green is insufficient: portability requires the same authentic
  slice through at least two independent adapters.
- Telos continuation is insufficient: mediation must enact typed dispatch and
  reconcile completion without personifying Telos.
- A deterministic controller being green is insufficient: it must carry a real
  cognitive program while leaving semantic judgment to attributed agents and
  separate adjudication.
- A model route being green is insufficient: complete substrate evidence includes
  generative/reasoning, vision, embedding, and ASR under genus-router/LiteLLM
  governance.
- A dashboard or terminal being green is insufficient: observability must derive
  from real persisted events, and attach must remain a separate adapter.
- A trained model looking promising is insufficient: philosophical confidence in
  the recurrent cognitive form does not transfer to GEH's empirical claims.
- A happy-path composition is insufficient: clean reconstruction, crash/replay,
  upgrade, rollback, and restoration are part of the product claim.
- `noetic-pi-docker` running is insufficient: it is the prior developed
  `noetic-pi` product instance, not noetic-dev's development/composition basis or
  proof that noetic-dev is complete.

Any roadmap, issue, PR, goalchain, or release claim that omits a matrix row must
name it as active/planned, condition it on an open question, or justify one of the
four true deferrals above. Silence is not a disposition, and sequencing is not
abeyance.
